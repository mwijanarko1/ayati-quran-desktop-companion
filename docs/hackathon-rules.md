# Quran Foundation Hackathon Rules

Last verified against the official hackathon page and terms on April 19, 2026.

Official sources:
- Hackathon page: <https://launch.provisioncapital.com/quran-hackathon>
- Terms and conditions: <https://launch.provisioncapital.com/quran-hackathon/terms>

## Event Summary
The Quran Foundation Hackathon is a Ramadan 2026 competition organized and sponsored by Provision Launch and Quran Foundation. It challenges participants to build technology that strengthens people's connection with the Quran, especially by helping users maintain Quran engagement after Ramadan.

Projects should improve accessibility, deepen understanding, build habits, or introduce new ways to engage with the Quran using the Quran Foundation API ecosystem.

## Who Can Participate
- Developers
- Designers
- Students
- Entrepreneurs
- Vibe coders
- Product managers
- Researchers
- OSS contributors

The competition is open worldwide. Teams can be solo or up to 4 members.

Additional eligibility and team rules from the official terms:
- Each individual may participate on only one team.
- Each team may submit only one project.
- Team composition must be finalized at submission.
- One team member must be the primary contact for organizer communications.
- Participants must provide accurate and truthful registration and submission information.

## Timeline
- Announcement and kickoff: Ramadan 2026
- Development period: Ramadan through Shawwal 1447
- Submission deadline: Early Dhu al-Hijjah 1447, May 20, 2026
- Judging period: 1 to 2 weeks after the deadline
- Winners announced after judging

## Technical Requirement
Projects must use at least one API from each category.

### Content API Requirement
Use at least one Quran Foundation Content API or Quran MCP.

Examples from the brief:
- Quran APIs
- Audio APIs
- Tafsir APIs
- Translation APIs
- Post APIs for lessons and reflections

### User API Requirement
Bookmarks
Collections
Streak Tracking
Post APIs (Post a reflection)
Activity & Goals APIs

Examples from the brief:
- Bookmarks
- Collections
- Streak tracking
- Post APIs for reflections
- Activity and goals APIs

## Submission Checklist
- Project title
- Team member names
- Short description
- Detailed explanation of the idea
- Live demo or working app link
- GitHub repository, if available
- 2 to 3 minute demo video
- API usage description

Submissions must be received before the deadline. The official terms state late submissions will not be accepted. Incomplete submissions may be deemed ineligible at the organizers' discretion.

## Judging Criteria
Projects are scored out of 100 points:

- Impact on Quran engagement: 30 points
- Product quality and UX: 20 points
- Technical execution: 20 points
- Innovation and creativity: 15 points
- Effective use of APIs: 15 points

## What Judges Care About
- Does the app help users connect more deeply with the Quran?
- Is the product usable, accessible, and polished?
- Is the code stable and functional?
- Does the idea feel fresh?
- Are the Quran Foundation APIs used meaningfully?

## Judging Process
- Phase 1: eligibility screening against technical and submission requirements.
- Phase 2: qualified submissions are independently scored by judges from Quran Foundation, Provision Launch, and technology or design professionals.
- If there is a tie, the project with the higher Impact on Quran Engagement score ranks higher.
- Judging decisions are final and binding.
- Judges may recuse themselves where there is a conflict of interest.
